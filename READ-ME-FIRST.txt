HOW TO INSTALL
1. Drag everything in this folder (app, lib, supabase, vercel.json) into your my-website project and
   choose "Replace" when asked. The folder layout matches your project, so files land in the right place.

2. Paste supabase/full-setup.sql into the Supabase SQL editor and run it once (safe to re-run).
   It already includes the new columns for Complete + Training Rooms.

3. Training Rooms 1-5 are already built in - nothing to set. (Optional: set DISCORD_TRAINING_ROOM_IDS to a
   comma-separated list of voice channel IDs if you ever want different rooms.)

4. Make something call  /api/trainings/room-check  every minute (cron-job.org is free).
   If you set CRON_SECRET, send the header:  Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>

WHAT'S IN IT
- Public Order 1/2/3, separate TPAC, and Pursuit Tactical Advisor training templates
- Midnight (UK time) clean-up of finished trainings from the Discord channel (vercel.json cron)
- Complete button on View Trainings -> DMs the instructor "did anyone leave?" -> pick who -> removed from their record
- Trainer panel > Training Bars: bar someone from ONE training (e.g. IPP) until a new one of that training is posted
- Role requests no longer post in the patrol channel
- Training rooms assigned automatically; instructor DM sent when everyone has left the room

NOTE: vercel.json replaces yours. It keeps your two existing crons and adds the cleanup ones.
If you've changed vercel.json since uploading, merge the two "/api/trainings/cleanup" lines in by hand.
